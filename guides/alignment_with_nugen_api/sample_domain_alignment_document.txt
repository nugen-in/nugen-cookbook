# The Secret of the Old Library

In a busy town surrounded by tall buildings and crowded streets, there lived a sixteen-year-old girl named Meera. She lived with her grandmother in a small house near the town market. Meera was a curious student who loved solving puzzles, exploring new places, and reading adventure books.

One afternoon, while returning from school, Meera noticed an old building hidden behind a row of trees. The building had broken windows, a wooden door, and a faded sign that read “Public Library.” She had lived in the town for years but had never noticed the building before.

Curious, she pushed the door gently. It creaked open, revealing a large room filled with dusty books, wooden shelves, and old tables. Sunlight entered through a small window, illuminating the dust floating in the air.

An elderly man sitting behind a desk looked up.

“Can I help you?” he asked.

“I didn't know this library existed,” Meera replied. “Why is nobody here?”

The man introduced himself as Mr. Sharma, the librarian. He explained that the library had once been popular, but most people had stopped visiting after a modern library opened across town. Since then, the old building had been slowly forgotten.

Meera walked between the shelves and discovered a book with a dark blue cover. Unlike the other books, it had no title on its front. When she opened it, she found a handwritten note on the first page.

“Knowledge is the greatest treasure, but only those who ask the right questions will find it.”

Beneath the message was a small drawing of a key.

Meera showed the note to Mr. Sharma. He looked surprised and explained that the library's founder had supposedly hidden a special collection of books somewhere in the building. Nobody had discovered it, and over time, people had begun to believe the story was only a legend.

Meera decided to investigate.

For the next few days, she visited the library after school. She examined the shelves, read old records, and searched for clues. Most of her classmates thought she was wasting her time, but she continued patiently.

One evening, she noticed that several books about history had been arranged in an unusual order. Their first letters formed the word “CLOCK.”

She looked around the room and found an old grandfather clock standing in the corner. Behind it was a small wooden panel. When she removed the panel, she discovered a metal key.

The key opened a locked cabinet beneath the librarian's desk.

Inside the cabinet was an old map showing a hidden staircase behind the tallest bookshelf. Meera and Mr. Sharma moved the shelf carefully and discovered a narrow staircase leading to a small underground room.

The room contained hundreds of books, including rare scientific discoveries, historical records, handwritten stories, and journals written by people who had lived in the town centuries earlier.

But the greatest surprise was a letter from the library's founder.

The letter explained that the collection was meant to be shared with everyone, especially children who could not afford to buy books. The founder believed that education should never be limited by wealth or social position.

Meera and Mr. Sharma decided to restore the collection and reopen the library. Meera invited her classmates, while Mr. Sharma contacted volunteers to repair the building. Local residents donated books, furniture, and money for repairs.

Within a few months, the old library became popular again. Children gathered there every afternoon to read, study, and discuss their ideas. Some discovered a love for science, while others began writing their own stories.

Meera was happy that a forgotten place had become an important part of the community once more.

Years later, when Meera became a scientist, she returned to the library to give a speech. Standing in the same room where her adventure had begun, she thanked Mr. Sharma for helping her discover not just a hidden collection of books, but also the power of curiosity.

She understood that the greatest discoveries often begin with a simple question.

Moral of the story: Curiosity, patience, and a love of learning can uncover valuable opportunities. Knowledge becomes even more powerful when it is shared with others.
